PULSE DEVNET

Trade attention.

Go long or short on what the world is playing right now. Live player counts, on-chain every minute. No expiry, no liquidations, no house.

How it works

Two pools per market. Longs in one, shorts in the other. When the player count moves, value flows from the losing pool to the winning pool, times the market's leverage.

Nobody can owe what isn't there. Winners are paid only from the other side's pool, so there are no liquidations and no liquidity providers to fund.

Fair fills. Orders fill at the price after the next update, so nobody can trade on a number they already saw.
